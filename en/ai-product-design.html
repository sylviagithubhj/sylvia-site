AI Product Design

Four weeks connecting AI to real design work, from research analysis to Figma- and code-based prototypes.

You set up an AI workspace that understands your project's context and turn research findings into screens and features. For designers and PMs who already use Figma and have tried making screens with AI.

Length4 weeks
FormatLive + recorded
FeedbackEvery week
ForSome Figma and AI screen-making experience

Curriculum

Week 1An AI workspace that understands your projectWrite down product goals, users and design principles so you stop re-explaining the background. Set up Claude Projects and custom instructions, plus prompts and review criteria for repeat work.
Week 2Finding design rationale in the research you already haveSort and compare interviews, user feedback and competitor material with AI. Check the analysis against the source to filter out guesses, and turn core problems and hypotheses into design requirements.
Week 3Connecting Figma and AI to build screens and componentsPractice a design workflow with Claude Code and Figma MCP. Build screens from your existing design context and components, then review repeated elements and state-specific UI.
Week 4Building and improving a testable prototypeBuild the core user flow in Cursor. Set test tasks and metrics, and find what to improve from how users respond.

You leave with

  • A working prototype you can show
  • Feedback on every assignment
  • Prompt and project templates
  • A cohort to keep building with
